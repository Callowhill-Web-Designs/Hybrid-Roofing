This Spring Garden home received a complete siding transformation with custom Hardie board and batten siding and matching soffit installation. The board and batten style adds timeless architectural character while providing the superior durability and weather resistance of James Hardie fiber cement products. Our team carefully removed the old siding, addressed any underlying issues, and installed the new Hardie siding with precision craftsmanship. The custom soffit work ensures proper ventilation while maintaining a cohesive aesthetic. This low-maintenance solution will protect the home for decades while maintaining its fresh appearance with minimal upkeep.
