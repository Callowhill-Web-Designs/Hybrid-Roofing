We completed an extensive stucco remediation and exterior renovation at this Fishtown property, transforming it with premium materials and custom features. The project included removal of failing stucco and installation of durable Hardie siding, energy-efficient Provia windows and doors, a waterproof Duradeck roof deck surface, a custom aluminum pergola for outdoor living, and elegant wrought iron railings. This comprehensive approach addressed all moisture concerns while dramatically enhancing the home's curb appeal and functionality. Our skilled craftsmen coordinated each phase seamlessly, ensuring proper integration of all systems for lasting performance and beauty.
