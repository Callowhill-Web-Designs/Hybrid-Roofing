This Mt. Airy home underwent a comprehensive exterior upgrade with complete siding replacement and a full deck rebuild featuring waterproof Duradeck roofing. We removed the deteriorating siding and installed durable, low-maintenance materials that provide superior weather protection and enhanced curb appeal. Our coordinated approach ensured both the siding and deck work seamlessly together, delivering a cohesive, professional result that significantly increases the home's value and enjoyment.
