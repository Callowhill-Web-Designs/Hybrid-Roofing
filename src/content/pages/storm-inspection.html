Storm Damage Gets Worse — Fast

Even minor storm damage can quickly lead to expensive repairs if left unchecked. Here's why acting quickly matters:

  • Hidden Damage You Can't See

    Hail, wind, and heavy rain cause damage that's invisible from the ground. Cracked seals, lifted shingles, and compromised flashing often go unnoticed until a leak forms, sometimes months later.

  • Insurance Claim Deadlines

    Most homeowners insurance policies require you to file storm damage claims within a specific window. Waiting too long can mean losing coverage you've already paid for.

  • Small Leaks Become Big Problems

    A pinhole leak or a few lifted shingles can allow water intrusion that damages insulation, drywall, and structural framing. Catching it early saves thousands.
