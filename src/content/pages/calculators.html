Roofing Types Explained

Torchdown: A modified bitumen roofing system applied with a torch. Durable and ideal for flat or low-slope roofs. Common in commercial and residential flat roofs.
Shingles: Traditional asphalt shingles, the most common residential roofing material. Great for sloped roofs with classic appearance and reliable protection.
TPO: Thermoplastic Polyolefin, a single-ply roofing membrane. Energy-efficient white roofing ideal for flat commercial roofs and modern homes.
